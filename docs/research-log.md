## Dating and access notes from this pass

UNESCO descriptions and brief syntheses provide broad calendar windows; this is not a replacement for site-specific excavation and chronology reports. Century bounds are explicit coarse windows. Taos remains a living community: its formation window does not imply abandonment. Copán’s arrival marker preserves the UNESCO page’s explicit 427 CE attribution. Institutional records can contain broad or conflicting chronology and should be compared with later specialist work.

[Poverty Point’s NPS article](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/monumental-earthworks-of-poverty-point-world-heritage-site.htm) supplies explicit 1700–1100 BCE dates, avoiding an unsupported conversion of UNESCO’s differing BP wording.

The [Rasmussen et al. GICC05 chronology](https://doi.org/10.1029/2005JD006079), Table 4 and footnote a, supplies ages before **2000 CE** and maximum counting errors. Those estimates exclude possible layer-identification bias; the timeline does not present them as standard deviations. The record within the 8.2 ka cold event is a dated horizon, not a claimed onset. This edition preserves that named 2006 chronology rather than claiming it is the latest revision.

[NOAA’s Younger Dryas sheet](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-01/3%20The%20Younger%20Dryas%20-Jan%202023.pdf) is an explanatory record with no separate timeline marker. Its discussion of freshwater routing is distinct from the attributed impact hypothesis. The Carlson et al. reference in that climate literature is not a citation to Randall Carlson.

Firestone et al. (2007) was checked through its [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17901202/); Holliday et al. (2023) through the [University of Vienna repository abstract](https://ucrisportal.univie.ac.at/en/publications/comprehensive-refutation-of-the-younger-dryas-impact-hypothesis-y/). The 2024 Sweatman–Powell–West response is **bibliographically verified only**, through the [author’s university research list](https://blogs.ed.ac.uk/martinsweatman/2022/05/20/research-outputs/); its citation discloses unavailable publisher full text. These are a selected historical exchange, not a comprehensive or current consensus review. Track assignment is editorial organization, not a quality rating for a journal or researcher.

[Waitt’s USGS report](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr83671) is placed at its 1983 publication year. Its older age estimates were not silently converted into calibrated calendar dates. The report documents regional repeated megafloods; it does not establish Atlantis or identify a biblical flood.

## Source practice

Institutional records come from UNESCO, English Heritage, the British Museum, the Israel Museum, the British Library, the Metropolitan Museum of Art, NASA, the US National Archives, the US Holocaust Memorial Museum, and the Knesset document archive. The Order of Malta supplies its own account of its emblem. Religious and philosophical accounts link to the source texts, including numbered Urantia paragraphs and Ra questions. Hancock entries use his official website, and Donnelly entries use a public-domain book edition.

Summaries are short original paraphrases. This collection does not reproduce complete copyrighted books or articles. Two museum photographs have public-domain records; other images are explicitly illustrative. Source metadata and rights are recorded in the project.

`citation.checkedOn` records the date the attribution was checked. It is not a claim that a narrative is historically established. A reference may support the narrative while its calendar placement requires a separate inference; `dateBasis` makes that distinction explicit.

## Deliberate limits

- Genesis does not supply BCE years for the indexed narratives. These passages remain unplaced. The earlier illustrative Ussher date has been removed.
- The linked Plato edition does not establish a composition year. The original approximate composition marker has been removed; the Atlantis narrative retains its explicitly interpreted internal chronology.
- Ra ages are anchored to the relevant recording year, 1981. Urantia’s Adam chronology uses the text’s explicit 1934 anchor. The Melchizedek conversion also cites the book’s own Jesus birth date.
- The English Chauvet listing gives BP ages without enough calibration context for Project Timeline. Its art record is unplaced; the modern discovery has a separate dated record.
- The British Museum Egyptian chronology was checked through its institutional search-index excerpt; the full page was unavailable. Its citations disclose this limitation.
- Hancock entries based on author synopses do not invent print page numbers. Rounded ages without a stated reference year remain unplaced.
- The Carlson record indexes the published title and description of episode 024. It does not claim that the full recording or timestamped statements were reviewed.
- The oldest displayed archaeological interval and a spiritual narrative’s much older dates are different kinds of records. The display’s year-by-year precision does not upgrade a source’s certainty.
- The symbol contexts are initial examples, not complete histories. The Syrian cross record is artifact-specific and notes modern restoration. Medieval Templar forms and other cross variants still need artifact-level sources.
