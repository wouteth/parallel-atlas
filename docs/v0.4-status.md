### What was added

- **Norse:** 22 passages from Anderson’s Gylfaginning, covering creation, first humans, Asgard, Baldr, Loki, Fimbulwinter and regeneration. The original Ragnarok ID remains; its chapter citation was corrected to XVI–XVII.
- **Hindu:** 22 Vishnu Purana records, covering the yugas, manvantara, Brahma’s day/night, Varaha, ocean churning, Dhruva, Prithu, Prahlada, Krishna, Dvaraka and Kalki. Manu’s Satapatha flood remains a separate text. Durations are not absolute dates.
- **Tolkien:** 18 additional narrative milestones from Christopher Tolkien’s official editorial introductions, including Númenor and later ages. These are introduction-level attributions, not a claim of full novel-chapter review. The original First Age ID was retained and improved instead of duplicated.
- **Jan Val Ellam:** nine narrative records across the official synopses of Terra Atlantis I, II and III. Each identifies a specific synopsis paragraph. Full chapters and calendar anchors remain unavailable in this review.
- **Terra Papers:** 14 passages from an attributed transcription, including Eridanus, the Sirian/Orion conflict, Tiamat, Eridu, EA/NIN-HUR-SAG, workers, exile and flood survival. Printed page markers are preserved; the 1996 edition has not been collated. Bek’Ti’s introductory story is included without inventing a verified 1947 date.
- **Sitchin:** 25 selected entries from an attributed Earth Chronicles time-chart transcription. Fifteen explicit BCE dates are plotted; ten “Years Ago” entries remain unanchored because the table supplies no reference year. This is not exhaustive coverage of the Earth Chronicles books.
- **Deep time:** 13 Urantia records from Papers 57–58, three Ra/Maldek records from Session 10, and five scientific records covering Earth/Moon formation, early life, extinction and Homo sapiens.

### Research still needed

Full primary-edition collation of Terra Papers and Sitchin; full Terra Atlantis chapters; Tolkien novel passages and variant chronologies; additional Hindu texts and Eddic versions; broader coverage of the original tracks; the original Carlson Halloween recording and timestamp. The prompt does not define a finite event list for whole authors or traditions, so “all their data” cannot honestly mean these books are exhaustively indexed. This release covers every named track and expands the stated comparison themes.

The older 0.4.4 audit found 213 records, with Tolkien/Hindu/Norse at one each and Jan Val Ellam/Terra Papers/Sitchin empty. Those gaps motivated this expansion.

## Corrections retained in the implementation

- **Lucifer Rebellion:** [Urantia 53:4.1](https://www.urantia.org/urantia-book-standardized/paper-53-lucifer-rebellion) says about 200,000 years ago. The brief’s 300,000-year premise is not used for dating.
- **Ragnarok:** the cited [Younger Edda](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/18947/18947-h/18947-h.htm), Gylfaginning, supplies no equivalent BCE/CE date. A shared absolute date is unestablished.
- **Jesus:** [Urantia 122:8.1](https://www.urantia.org/urantia-book-standardized/paper-122-birth-and-infancy-jesus) gives 7 BCE, already reflected in the catalog. The brief’s 4 BCE and “year zero” are not substituted for source chronology.
- **Atlantis and geology:** 9600 BCE is approximately 11,600 years ago, not 9,600 years ago. Plato’s date is narrative time converted using an approximate Solon anchor. Younger Dryas onset/end and Meltwater Pulse 1B are distinct; Quest 2 preserves the research needed.
- **Urantia and Atlantis:** the submerged-Eden record is not automatically renamed Atlantis or counted as an explicit Atlantis reference.
- **Eridu / Eridanus / EA:** comparisons now cite the Terra Papers transcription; its printed edition and the proposed etymology remain unverified. Shared names do not establish independent corroboration.
- **Law of One:** [76.13–14](https://www.lawofone.info/s/76#13) gives durations. These remain readable without invented absolute starting dates.
- **Tolkien:** authored fiction has its own evidence label and is not treated as independent ancient testimony.

## Usability update — 0.4.1

A browser-driven pass adds a compact research workspace, topic shortcuts, a track picker, direct account-list access, larger timeline cards and searchable clusters. Custom date ranges and URL-backed filters/tracks preserve context through records and reloads. Ordinary scroll remains page scrolling; mobile timeline movement is opt-in. Map focus separates crowded controls while retaining coordinate anchors; selected details are easier to reach. See HANDOFF for the validation scope.

## Read-only static update — 0.4.2

The owner explicitly removed all user mutations from scope. The server, authentication dependency, database adapter, personal library, saved combinations, API calls and publishing forms are gone. Research content and specialist notes are repository files. `vercel.json` publishes only the static `dist/` output. Filter and navigation state use the URL, without browser-storage writes.

## Acceptance evidence

Date/content/layout unit tests, production TypeScript/build and schema-4 export are retained. Browser tests run against the built static output without API mocks, including checks for no API or non-read requests, no browser-storage/database writes, and no retired account/editing flows. Responsive checks cover 320–1280px. The personal Vercel preview is live and was checked in the browser. Physical devices and specialist contributions remain unverified.

The declined confidence heat map and separate top-similarities ranking page were not built. Existing permanent IDs were preserved. The public demo is https://project-timeline-wouteth.vercel.app. The owner authorized the GitHub and personal Vercel renames, source push and deployment on September 14, 2026. Community outreach and payment configuration remain separate work.
