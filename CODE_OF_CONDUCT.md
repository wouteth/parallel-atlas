# Code of Conduct

Parallel Atlas welcomes people with different backgrounds, beliefs, and levels of experience. These expectations apply in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and any project-managed community spaces.

## Participate with care

- Treat people with respect, including when discussing religion, culture, identity, and disputed historical claims.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and methods rather than a person's identity or motives.
- Cite sources, acknowledge uncertainty, correct mistakes, and distinguish your interpretation from the source's claims.
- Give constructive feedback, make room for newcomers, and respect requests to end an unproductive exchange.
- Respect privacy and the rights of people whose work or cultural heritage is discussed.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and repeated targeted disruption are unacceptable. Disagreement with an interpretation is not itself misconduct.

## Reporting and moderation

For visible misconduct, flag the relevant content to [@wouteth](https://github.com/wouteth) if it is safe to do so. Avoid reposting abusive material or personal information. For a private platform report, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls; those reports go to GitHub, not to a dedicated project moderation inbox. The project does not currently publish a private conduct-reporting address.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, request a change in behavior, lock a discussion, or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the severity, context, and recurrence of the behavior; serious threats or privacy violations may warrant immediate restrictions. Explain moderation decisions when doing so will not expose private information or cause further harm.

If you believe a project moderation decision was mistaken, request reconsideration with relevant context through an available project thread when safe. Concerns involving the maintainer or GitHub policy violations can be reported directly to GitHub through its reporting controls. Retaliation against someone raising a concern is unacceptable.
