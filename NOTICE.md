# Project and third-party rights

The MIT license applies to original project code and original project-authored documentation/content. It does not relicense third-party books, quoted passages, photographs, fonts, dependency code or trademarks. Source citations identify works for research; linking a work does not grant reproduction rights.

Dependencies retain the licenses distributed in their packages. The local lockfile identifies installed versions. Google Fonts, illustrative Unsplash photographs and museum images retain their respective terms; see README and `src/data/images.ts` for asset attribution and limitations.

The bundled Natural Earth land geometry is public domain; its source and terms are recorded in `public/maps/README.md`. Original source publications remain external and are not bundled as full texts.

Public GitHub visibility and deployment require separate owner authorization. The presence of a license does not mean this private pre-launch edition has been published.

Book coverage metadata added in 0.4.6 cites the primary online editions directly. The short chapter headings from H. H. Wilson’s 1840 Vishnu Purana and paragraph openings from Benjamin Jowett’s Plato translations are public-domain text, attributed in the interface. Poetic Edda event summaries are original paraphrases with Henry Adams Bellows’s 1923 stanza references. No full research books or downloaded PDFs are redistributed by this project.
